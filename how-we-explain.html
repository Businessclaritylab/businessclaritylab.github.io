Our Lens

We Put Complex Tools Under a Different Lens.

Software, systems and digital tools can sound complicated because they are often explained using technical language, buzzwords and features that don't mean much to the person trying to understand them. We take a different approach.

THE BUSINESS LENS APPROACH

Explain It Like You're Five.

Not because our readers aren't intelligent. Quite the opposite. We believe people should not need to understand complicated language before they can understand a simple idea.

01

Put It Under the Lens

We start with the tool, system or technology itself and look at what it is really designed to do.

e.g. Pipedrive for example, is marketed as "A global hyper-growth sales ecosystem designed to optimize pipeline throughput." →

02

Remove the Jargon

Technical terms and marketing buzzwords can make simple ideas sound complicated. We strip away the unnecessary language.

e.g. "A global hyper-growth sales ecosystem designed to optimize pipeline throughput." →

03

Make It Click

We explain the idea in ordinary language, using comparisons and explanations that make you think, “Oh. That's what it is.”

e.g. It’s simply a digital scoreboard for your sales team that makes sure you never forget to follow up on a deal. ✅ →

04

Show You a Real Example

We take the explanation out of theory and show how the tool or system can actually be used in the real world.

e.g. An owner sees which customer hasn't been called in 3 days and fixes it with one click before the lead goes cold. 🤝

PRACTICAL EXAMPLES

Because “What Does It Actually Do?” Matters.

We don't stop at definitions. We want you to be able to picture what the technology actually does in everyday business.

CRM Software

Instead of simply saying “customer relationship management platform,” we explain it as a place where a business keeps track of its customers, conversations, follow-ups and sales.

e.g. A digital receptionist.

Fleet Management Software

Rather than drowning you in technical features, we show how the system can help a transport business keep track of vehicles, drivers, routes, maintenance and activity.

e.g. A logistics coordinator.

AI Tools

We look past the buzzwords and explain what the tool can actually do, what information it needs, where it can help and where it may not be useful.

e.g. A virtual assistant.

Business Systems

We break systems down into the individual jobs they perform so you can understand how they fit into the way a business actually works.

e.g. An automated sales rep.

UNDER THE LENS

We Ask the Questions That Actually Matter.

Before anyone decides whether a tool is worth their time or money, they should understand what they are actually getting.

What Is It?

What is this tool or system in the simplest possible terms?
Cutting through the name to find the actual meaning.

What Does It Do?

What does it actually help a person or business do?
Because a list of features isn't a solution.

Who Is It For?

Which businesses, teams or individuals would genuinely benefit from it?
e.g. Marketing agencies, law firms, or shop owners.

How Does It Work?

What happens when you actually use it, explained without unnecessary technical language?
Opening the box so you aren't buying a mystery.

What Does It Cost?

What are you actually paying for, and are there important costs or limitations to understand?
Ensuring the price tag matches the value returned.

Is It Worth It?

Once you understand it, does it genuinely make sense for your needs?
The final investigation: profit over gimmick.
