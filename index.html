THE RESEARCH THESIS

Why most software advice costs business owners thousands.

Search for any business tool online and you will find hundreds of recycled checklists repeating whatever the vendor's homepage says. They tell you every platform is "scalable, intuitive, and AI-powered." They never tell you that a $12/seat project tool enforces a mandatory 3-seat minimum ($432/year), that a free CRM hides custom user permissions behind a sales quote, or that storing 25,000 past e-commerce customers on the wrong email platform quietly costs $4,000 extra over three years.

Inside Business Lens Lab, we treat every platform like a corporate hire. Before recommending a tool, we define the exact job it replaces (such as a Digital Receptionist, Sales Deal Closer, or Automated Bookkeeper), inspect its real interface, expose where it breaks down, and calculate the true three-year return on capital.
