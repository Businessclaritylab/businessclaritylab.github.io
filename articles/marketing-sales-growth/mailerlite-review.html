The Lab Glossary: Email Deliverability and Architecture

Deliverability: The percentage of outgoing emails that successfully land in the customer primary inbox rather than getting dumped into the spam or junk folder.
DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail): A digital cryptographic signature attached to your emails that proves to email providers like Google and Yahoo that the email genuinely came from your website and was not forged by a scammer.
SPF (Sender Policy Framework): A public security record on your domain name that lists the specific computer servers authorized to send emails on your behalf.
DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication): An advanced global security standard that tells receiving email servers what to do if an incoming message fails SPF or DKIM verification checks.
Double Opt-In: A best-practice subscription method where a new reader signs up and must click a confirmation link in their inbox before being added to your list, ensuring zero fake spam emails enter your database.
CTOR (Click-to-Open Rate): An elite metric measuring the percentage of people who opened your email and found your message so compelling that they clicked a link inside it.

03. The Mechanics: How Email Deliverability and Workflows Function

To operate email marketing with commercial success, a business owner must understand the invisible technological physics of the inbox. When you click Send, your email does not instantly appear on a customer phone. It passes through a gauntlet of algorithmic gatekeepers controlled by Google, Microsoft, and Apple.

MailerLite functions through three core engineering pillars:

1. Cryptographic Domain Authentication (The Security Badge): Email inbox providers are ruthless about fighting spam. If a server sends thousands of emails without proper verification, it gets blacklisted. MailerLite guides you through adding three security records to your domain name: SPF, DKIM, and DMARC. These records act like an unforgeable government passport proving that your store is legitimate, ensuring your emails bypass the spam folder and land directly in the primary inbox.

2. Visual Behavioral Automations (The Automated Salesperson): This is where human labor is permanently eliminated. In MailerLite, you construct visual automation sequences based on customer actions. For example:
*Trigger:* A customer downloads a free guide on your website.
*Action:* MailerLite delivers the guide immediately.
*Condition:* The system waits two days and checks if the subscriber clicked the download link. If they clicked, it tags them as an Active Lead and sends a follow-up educational story. If they did not click, it sends a gentle reminder with an alternative subject line.
This entire relationship sequence runs twenty-four hours a day without you touching a computer.

3. Subscriber Segmentation and Clean Lists: In traditional email software, sending to inactive subscribers ruins your reputation with Google. MailerLite provides automated list hygiene. It tracks subscriber engagement, allowing you to segment your audience into VIP buyers, active readers, and dormant leads, ensuring you never waste money sending messages to abandoned inboxes.
