Helping people understand before they invest.

Business Lens Lab exists to make business software, systems and digital tools easier to understand before people spend their money, time or attention on them.

WHY WE EXIST

Technology Should Be Easier to Understand

Businesses are surrounded by software and digital tools, but understanding what those tools actually do can be surprisingly difficult.

01

Too Many Choices

There are thousands of software platforms, systems and digital tools competing for attention. Choosing between them can quickly become overwhelming.

02

Too Much Jargon

Many technology explanations are written for people who already understand the technology. We believe useful information should be understandable without a technical background.

03

Marketing Isn't Understanding

A product description can tell you what a company wants you to know. We want to help you understand what the tool actually means in practical terms.

04

The Right Lens Comes First

Before deciding whether something is worth buying, you should first understand what it does, who it is for and where it fits.

OUR APPROACH

Understanding Before Investing

Business Lens Lab is built around a simple idea: better decisions start with better understanding.

Explain It Simply

We break down complicated concepts as simply as we can, using practical language and removing unnecessary technical jargon.
Think of us as your "Manual-to-Human" translator.

Use Real Examples

We connect ideas to situations people can recognise so that concepts become easier to understand and remember.
Fewer complex graphs, more real-life business stories.

Explore the Details

We look beyond surface-level descriptions to understand what tools, systems and technologies actually do and how they work in the real world.
Taking the lid off the "Black Box" of tech.

Help You Decide

The goal isn't simply to tell you what a product does. It is to help you decide whether it makes sense for your particular needs.
A software guide should be a compass, not a sales pitch.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Learn First. Decide Second.

We don't believe people should have to become technology experts before they can make sensible decisions about technology.

Business Lens Lab is designed to become a place where people can look at unfamiliar tools, technologies and business concepts through a simpler lens, understand what they actually mean, and then decide what to do with that knowledge.
